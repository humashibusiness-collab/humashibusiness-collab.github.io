Playable ad samples

HTML5 playable ads for mobile games. One build is exported for AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta, Google Ads, Mintegral, TikTok (Pangle), Moloco and Liftoff. Tap a sample to play it in your browser.

Monsters reach the village gate. Merge bats into a dragon, burn the horde, and then the boss appears. About 15 s.

How they are built 作り

  • Hook in the first seconds, one tutorial hand, a clear fail or win moment, an end card, and a Play button visible from start to end.
  • Portrait and landscape. Under 1 MB each.
  • Starts only when the ad is visible, never opens the store on the first tap, sound only after the first tap, nothing counts down before the first tap.
  • Automated tests for every network export. Checked in AppLovin's Playable Preview and Google's HTML5 Validator.
  • The art in these samples is AI-assisted. Client work uses the client's own game art.
